Where XRay.Office Differs

All 946 documents in the sample corpus were rendered twice — once by headless LibreOffice, once by XRay.Office — and compared page by page at 150 dpi. 754 came out clean. These are the other 192: every one that shows a pixel defect or paginates differently, worst first, each with the divergent page side by side and a reading of what actually went wrong.

Since this sweep, 26 fixes have landed and every document below is accounted for. 11 are measurably fixed against the same banked reference, 29 are reference-version divergences, 9 are the reference's own fault, and the remaining 143 are open with a named cause rather than a symptom — the largest being the advance-width divergence, which is architectural. Two root causes found here had no patch and were fixed directly: anchored frames painted in document order instead of by relativeHeight, which made five documents look like they had lost content they were in fact drawing; and an unset page zoom read as 1% rather than 100%, which drew a spreadsheet's header and footer at 0.120 pt. The pairs below are the sweep's renderings and are not re-rendered.

Revised twice, and the second pass needed no reference at all. An eight-lane source investigation moved 29 cases out of the defect list entirely — they are reference-version divergences, where XRay.Office is correct for the LibreOffice the project develops against and this sweep's reference is a release behind — re-tagged 3 as the reference's fault and corrected 11 readings the measurements disproved. A second pass then re-checked the catalogue against evidence that is a statement about our own output — what our PDF's content stream holds, in what order it paints, at what size — which is as true of 26.2 as of 24.2. That found one cause behind five separate readings (content drawn, then painted over), corrected 3 more, and upheld 7. Every correction carries its original text beneath it.

How to read this

The order is severity: documents with a pixel defect first, ranked by how badly the worst page's structure diverges, then the documents whose pages match but whose page counts do not. Every reading below is a description of what the two images show — not a diagnosis of the code behind it.
