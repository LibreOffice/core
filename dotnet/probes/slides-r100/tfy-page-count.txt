slides-r99/tfy.py finds a PDF's pages by scanning every `N 0 obj` and testing a
700-byte window for /Type /Page.  The window runs past `endobj` into the next object
whenever objects are packed tightly, so a dictionary that is not a page is read as one
and takes the FOLLOWING object's /Contents.  Every per-page pairing after the first such
artefact is then off by one or more, and the two legs need not be off by the same amount.
