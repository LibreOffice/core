# Reach predicted before the sweep finished

Written while the whole-track sweep at `e1243574e` was still running, so that the prediction
cannot be fitted to the answer.

The solidus rule fires only where a line break would land directly after a `/` **and** a blank
stands within the preceding 66 characters — so a wrapping cell holding a path, a URI, a
regulation reference or a date written with solidi, in a column narrow enough to break inside
it. A census would count documents holding a `/` and be useless: nearly every workbook does.

- **sheets: 10–25 of 171 documents change bytes, and 1–3 verdicts move.** The named target is
  `tk-syllabus-comparison-document-v5.xlsx`, 852/855 pages.
- **words and slides: fewer, because the rule needs a narrow measure.** A `/` in body text on a
  15 cm measure rarely lands at a line end. Guess 3–15 documents each, 0–2 verdicts.
