Which font height sizes a picture bullet's box: three variants, measured, none of which is right
on both decks.

The quantity is `nFontHeight` in
    nHeight = round( nFontHeight * 0.2540 * nBulletHeight )        svdfppt.cxx:3455
and the two candidates are the paragraph's own first portion (what
`GetNumberFormat(..., pParaObj, ...)` reads at :3705-3708) and the master char level's height at
that depth (what `PPTStyleSheet`'s constructor passes at :4409-4419 for the rule a paragraph
keeps when `nHardCount` is zero).

V3 is the source's own rule as far as this reader can evaluate it -- the seven `GetAttrib` calls
`GetNumberFormat` sums, mask 0x5F1: BulletOn, BulletChar, BulletFont, BulletHeight, BulletColor,
TextOfs, BulletOfs -- and it behaves as V2 on BOTH decks, because France's page-8 paragraphs
state none of the seven.  Their masks, traced out of this reader: 0x00001800, 0x00001801 and
0x00001000, i.e. LineFeed, Alignment and (on one) BulletOn.  So the reference takes the
paragraph's portion on a paragraph whose seven attributes are soft, which `nHardCount` alone
cannot explain.

What is left is one of the two terms of `PPTParagraphObj::GetAttrib` this reader cannot evaluate
-- a destination instance of TSS_Type::Unknown, and the comparison of the source instance's
master level against the destination instance's (svdfppt.cxx:6060-6137).  The same pair is
already recorded as unevaluable against `SlideParagraph.LineSpacingStated`.

V1 is kept: it is what the paragraph overload literally reads, it is exact on the deck this seat
is about (six bullets, position and size), and it is the variant that moves the registered
statistic.  Its cost is `ws_prod-g-doc-Events-Part-M-presentation` page 21.
