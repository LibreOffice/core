## 2. Root cause

**OMML is not implemented anywhere in `dotnet/src`.**
`git grep -il 'omath\|sSub\|officeDocument/2006/math' -- dotnet/src` returns
nothing but a font-name list in `SystemFontResolver.cs`. `[src]`

What draws the flat text is a **missing namespace guard** in the layout walk.
`DocxLayoutSource.RunWalker.Append` (`dotnet/src/XRay.Office.WordProcessing/Ooxml/
DocxLayoutSource.cs`:1912) switches on `child.Name.LocalName` alone — no
`NamespaceName` test anywhere in the method. So:

* `m:oMath`, `m:oMathPara`, `m:sSub`, `m:e`, `m:sub`, `m:f`, `m:num`, `m:den`,
  `m:rad`, `m:deg`, `m:d`, `m:nary`, `m:m`, `m:mr` all fall to
  `default: Append(child, depth + 1)` (`:2244`) and are walked straight through;
* `m:r` matches `case "r":` (`:2235`) and is treated as an ordinary `w:r` —
  and because an OMML run's formatting really is a `w:rPr` child, its
  `Cambria Math` font table entry is even picked up, which is why the flattened
  text is drawn in a substituted serif;
* `m:t` matches `case "t" when !Suppressed: Emit(child.Value);` (`:2065`).

The result is that every formula is emitted as **the concatenation of its `m:t`
leaves, in document order, at the body's size, on the body's baseline**. Nothing
generated by the construct itself — the fraction bar, the radical sign and its
vinculum, the delimiters (`m:begChr`/`m:endChr` are *attributes*), the n-ary
operator (`m:chr`), the accent (`m:chr`) — exists as an `m:t`, so all of it is
silently absent. `[src]`, `[bin]`

The **extraction** path is guarded and therefore does the opposite:
`DocxContentReader.ReadRuns` / `ReadRun` (`DocxContentReader.cs`:366, :446) both
`continue` on `child.Name.NamespaceName != OoxmlNamespaces.WordprocessingML`, so
`xray-office extract` drops every formula entirely. Measured on the fixture: the
extracted line reads `sub inline: before  after (bare subscript).` `[bin]`
So the two paths disagree — the drawing path draws text the extraction path
cannot see.

### What the reference does instead

`OOXMLFastContextHandlerMath::process` (`sw/source/writerfilter/ooxml/
OOXMLFastContextHandler.cxx`:2517-2559) creates an **embedded StarMath object**
(`SO3_SM_CLASSID`) per `m:oMath` and hands the buffered OMML to
`readFormulaOoxml`; `m:oMathPara` additionally carries its `m:jc` through
`mnMathJcVal`, defaulting to **centre** (`:185-187`). The formula is then laid
out by `starmath/` with `SmFormat`'s defaults, not by Writer's text layout. `[src]`

The tarball ships Math — `libsmlo.so`, `libsmdlo.so`, `share/registry/math.xcd`
are all present under `/opt/libreoffice26.2` — and 26.2.4.2 does render every
construct. `[bin]`

## 3. The numbers

### 3.1 The reference's subscript rule, and why "20 %" is the wrong constant

`SmFormat::SmFormat` (`starmath/source/format.cxx`:32, 40-41) sets
`vSize[SIZ_INDEX] = 60` and `vDist[DIS_SUBSCRIPT] = vDist[DIS_SUPERSCRIPT] = 20`. `[src]`

`SmSubSupNode::Arrange` (`starmath/source/node.cxx`:1143-1225) then, for `RSUB`:

1. scales the subscript to `SIZ_INDEX/100` of the body (`:1188`);
2. `nDist = nOrigHeight * DIS_SUBSCRIPT / 100` (`:1203-1204`);
3. aligns with `RectVerAlign::Bottom`, and `SmRect`'s `nAlignB` **is the
   baseline** (`starmath/source/rect.cxx`:196), so this is a *baseline* align;
4. adds `nDist`;
5. **clamps**: `nDelimLine = SmFromTo(GetAlignB(), GetAlignT(), 0.4)` with
   `nAlignT = nBaseline - 0.75 * nFontHeight` (`rect.cxx`:191), i.e. the
   delimiter line is `baseline − 0.30 × H`; if the subscript's *top* is above it
   the whole subscript is pushed down (`node.cxx`:1209-1211).

So the drop is `max(0.20·H, ascent_em·0.60·H − 0.30·H)`, and **the clamp wins**:
for Liberation Serif (`ascent/em = 1825/2048 = 0.89111`) the second arm is
`0.2347·H` against the first's `0.2000·H`. Anyone implementing the stated 20 %
alone lands the subscript **17 % too high**.

Measured, four independent cases, from PyMuPDF per-character `origin` (the
baseline the writer's `Td`/`Tm` stated — *not* `pdftotext -bbox`, whose ink box
carries a constant per-face offset that looks exactly like this defect):

## 9. Proposed patch

Not applied; no C# was modified and nothing was built.

### 9.1 The namespace constant

`dotnet/src/XRay.Office.Ooxml/OoxmlNamespaces.cs`, beside `WordprocessingML` (:16):

```csharp
/// <summary>Office MathML, the namespace an OOXML formula's elements live in.</summary>
public const string OfficeMath = "http://schemas.openxmlformats.org/officeDocument/2006/math";
```

### 9.2 The seat

`dotnet/src/XRay.Office.WordProcessing/Ooxml/DocxLayoutSource.cs`,
`RunWalker.Append(XElement element, int depth)` at **:1912**.

*Current behaviour.* The `foreach (XElement child in element.Elements())` loop
switches on `child.Name.LocalName` with **no namespace test**, so an OMML subtree
is walked as though it were WordprocessingML: `m:r` takes `case "r":` (:2235),
`m:t` takes `case "t" when !Suppressed:` (:2065), and every structural element
(`m:oMath`, `m:sSub`, `m:e`, `m:sub`, `m:f`, `m:num`, `m:den`, `m:rad`, `m:d`,
`m:nary`, `m:m`) falls to `default: Append(child, depth + 1);` (:2244). A formula
is therefore drawn as the concatenation of its `m:t` leaves at the paragraph's
size on the paragraph's baseline, and everything the construct *generates* is
absent.

*Corrected behaviour.* First statement inside the loop:

```csharp
if (child.Name.NamespaceName == OoxmlNamespaces.OfficeMath)
{
    AppendMath(child, depth + 1);
    continue;
}
```

`case "r":` and `case "t"` keep their meaning for WordprocessingML alone.
**Do not add the guard on its own**: with no `AppendMath` it turns wrong text
into no text, which loses the searchable formula content that is currently this
renderer's one advantage here.

### 9.3 `AppendMath` — stage 1, in the same file, no new layout engine

A recursive walk over the math subtree carrying `(Escapement escapement,
double scale)`, emitting through the existing `Emit`. `Escapement`
(`dotnet/src/XRay.Office.WordProcessing/Layout/Escapement.cs`) already carries
`Proportion` (size %) and `Percent` (offset as a percentage of the font's
*height*, ascent + descent + line gap) and is the right carrier.

| element | stage-1 handling |
|---|---|
| `m:oMath` | recurse; set the run's size to **12 pt** rather than the paragraph's (§8) |
| `m:oMathPara` | recurse, and centre the paragraph unless `m:oMathParaPr/m:jc` says otherwise — `OOXMLFastContextHandler.cxx`:185-198 defaults it to `CENTER` |
| `m:r`, `m:t` | as today (`w:rPr` inside `m:r` is genuinely WordprocessingML) |
| `m:e` | recurse at the current escapement |
| `m:sub` | recurse with size × **0.60** and baseline **lowered** by `max(0.20·em, ascent_em·0.60·em − 0.30·em)` |
| `m:sup` | same size, baseline **raised** by the superscript arm of the same rule |
| `m:d` | emit `m:dPr/m:begChr@m:val` (default `(`) before the `m:e`s, `m:endChr` (default `)`) after, `m:sepChr` (default `\|`) between |
| `m:rad` | emit `U+221A` before the radicand; emit `m:deg` first at 0.60 size raised, unless `m:degHide` is `1` |
| `m:nary` | emit `m:naryPr/m:chr@m:val` (default `U+222B`) at **1.45×** size; emit `m:sub`/`m:sup` at 0.60 lowered/raised unless `m:subHide`/`m:supHide` |
| `m:f` | emit numerator, then `/`, then denominator — until stage 2 stacks them |
| `m:acc` | emit the `m:e`, then `m:accPr/m:chr@m:val` (default `U+0302`) as a combining mark |
| `m:func` | emit `m:fName` then `m:e` |
| `m:m`, `m:mr` | emit rows separated by a line break, cells by a space |
| `m:*Pr`, `m:ctrlPr` | skip |

The subscript constant is the one thing to get right and the one a reading of the
specification gets wrong. `SmFormat` states `DIS_SUBSCRIPT = 20`
(`starmath/source/format.cxx`:41), but `SmSubSupNode::Arrange`
(`starmath/source/node.cxx`:1199-1211) then clamps the subscript's *top* to the
delimiter line at `baseline − 0.30 × em`, and **for every Latin face the clamp
wins**: with Liberation Serif's `ascent/em = 0.89111` the drop is `0.2347·em`,
not `0.2000·em`. Implementing the stated 20 % puts the subscript **17 % too
high** — 0.42 pt at 12 pt, four times a rendering comparison's tolerance.
Confirmed against 26.2.4.2 at four sites, worst error 0.07 pt (§3.1).

Expressed through `Escapement`, whose `Percent` is of the font *height* rather
than the em: `Percent = -(drop / fontHeight) × 100`, which is **−20** for
Liberation Serif and is **not** a face-independent constant — compute it from the
face rather than hard-coding it. `Proportion = 60` (StarMath's `SIZ_INDEX`), not
the 58 that `Escapement.AutomaticProportion` carries for `w:vertAlign`.

### 9.4 Stage 2 — the part that needs a box model

Stacking a fraction over a rule, drawing a radical's vinculum, putting an n-ary
operator's limits above and below it, laying a matrix out in rows and columns,
and stretching a delimiter to its content all need a 2-D math box with its own
baseline, and cannot be done by emitting into the paragraph's character stream.
§3.2 and §8 give the target geometry for each, measured; `starmath/source/node.cxx`
`SmBinVerNode::Arrange` (:831), `SmRootNode::Arrange` (:725) and
`SmBinHorNode::Arrange` (:786) are the rules, and `starmath/source/format.cxx`:30-60
the constants. The reference draws its radical sign from **OpenSymbol**, stretched
per instance.
